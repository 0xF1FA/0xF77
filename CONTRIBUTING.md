# Contributing
*The graveyard is open.*

We welcome those willing to exhume forgotten computational behavior, challenge old assumptions, and document what they uncover.

## The Ritual

1. **Excavate.** Find something strange, broken, forgotten, or unexplored.
2. **Reanimate.** Build the smallest experiment that demonstrates it.
3. **Witness.** Record the compiler, environment, inputs, outputs, and failures.
4. **Preserve.** Submit your findings with reproducible evidence.

## Laws of the Crypt

- Evidence outranks enthusiasm.
- Failed experiments deserve tombstones, not erasure.
- Distinguish native behavior from machinery supplied by the host.
- Never bury limitations or exaggerate discoveries.
- Bring no secrets, credentials, or unrelated private materials into the crypt.
